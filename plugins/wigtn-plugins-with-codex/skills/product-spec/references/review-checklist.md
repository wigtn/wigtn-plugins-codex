# PRD Review Checklist

## Product contract

- The problem, target user, outcome, and non-goals agree with one another.
- Requirements have stable IDs and one testable responsibility each.
- Acceptance criteria are externally observable and map back to requirements.
- Terms, roles, entities, and states are used consistently.

## Feasibility

- Repository constraints, existing behavior, integrations, migrations, and ownership boundaries are acknowledged.
- Solution detail is supported by evidence rather than invented scale.
- Dependencies and rollout or compatibility risks are visible.

## UX and operations

- Relevant loading, empty, error, success, authorization, recovery, and destructive states exist.
- Analytics, observability, security, privacy, and accessibility appear only where the product needs them.
- Open decisions name an owner or a decision point when known.

Report findings by impact: blocker, high, medium, low. Avoid numeric quality scores.
