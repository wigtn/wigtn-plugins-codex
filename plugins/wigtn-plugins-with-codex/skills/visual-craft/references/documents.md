# Document craft

Use for writing or substantially refining documents, reports and slides. Follow
the requested format and existing publication or company template. Plain Markdown
is a valid final artifact; do not add a cover, executive summary or slide deck by default.

## Narrative and evidence

- Organize around the reader's question and the material available. For a technical
  report, make the question, method, result, limitations and resulting decision easy
  to find. These are content needs, not mandatory headings or a fixed file bundle.
- Keep connected reasoning in paragraphs. Use lists for genuinely parallel items,
  ordered steps for sequences and tables for comparisons. Avoid turning every
  sentence into its own paragraph or every paragraph into a titled section.
- Keep the evidence beside the claim. Preserve sources, units, denominators,
  conditions, uncertainty and caveats. Do not shorten away a limitation or turn a
  proposal into a measured result for a cleaner page.
- Use direct, natural Korean when requested. Keep terms such as API, UI, frontend
  or benchmark when familiar to the audience; explain unfamiliar terms once.
  Match the user's register without mechanically repeating polite endings,
  grand conclusions, rhetorical contrasts or promotional adjectives.
- In new copy, prefer sentence structure over em-dash interruptions and decorative
  separators. Never silently rewrite quotations, code, equations, identifiers,
  citations or legal wording to satisfy a visual preference.

## Layout by format

- Markdown: use meaningful heading levels and readable paragraphs; avoid manual
  line breaks, blank-line padding and HTML styling merely to control appearance.
- Paginated documents: keep a heading with its following content, make table headers
  usable across pages, and inspect breaks around figures, captions and source notes.
  Remove redundant prose or adjust layout before shrinking body text.
- Slides: give each slide a clear audience-facing message. Split genuinely separate
  messages when scope permits; preserve material detail in readable supporting
  slides or notes as appropriate. Do not add slides solely to satisfy a word quota.
- Charts and tables: preserve exact values, scales and units. Choose the display
  for the comparison, not for decorative variety. Dense appendices and data tables
  are legitimate when needed.

## Verify the delivered artifact

Review the final text for fidelity and repeated filler. For visual formats, use the
host workflow to inspect rendered pages or slides for clipping, font substitution,
Korean line breaks, stranded headings, unreadable footnotes and table overflow.
For web documents, inspect narrow and wide layouts. For plain Markdown, check
structure, links and reading flow; do not force an export solely for this skill.
Report only checks actually performed. Follow any stricter rendering requirements
of the format-specific workflow without duplicating its checks.
